I am a senior research physicist at [CERN](https://home.cern), working on the
[CMS experiment](https://cms.cern) at the Large Hadron Collider since 2007.
My research focuses on **artificial intelligence for particle physics**: using
deep learning to extend the sensitivity of LHC experiments to new physics, with a
particular emphasis on **real-time AI** running on FPGAs in the trigger system.

### Career

- **CERN** — on CMS since 2007; CERN Fellow, then research staff physicist.
- **University of Wisconsin–Madison** — BaBar experiment at SLAC.
- **University of Rome** — BaBar experiment; UTfit collaboration.

### Research highlights

- Anomaly-detection triggers for new physics at the LHC, culminating in the
  AXOL1TL and CICADA algorithms deployed in the CMS Level-1 trigger.
- Co-founder of [hls4ml](https://github.com/fastmachinelearning/hls4ml) and the
  *Fast Machine Learning* community.
- Graph neural networks for jet tagging and reconstruction (JEDI-net, GarNet).
- Searches for supersymmetry and exotica in CMS (razor variables, dijet and diboson resonances).
- CKM Unitarity Triangle fits with UTfit.

### Roles, grants and awards

- CMS task leader / contact in CERN's [Next Generation Triggers](https://home.cern/smarter-decisions-at-the-speed-of-collisions/) project (WP1, WP4).
- CMS coordinator of the Physics Performance and Datasets (PPD) area; member of CMS Physics Coordination.
- ERC Consolidator Grant *mPP* (2018–2023) on deep learning for high-energy physics;
  ERC Proof-of-Concept grant on hls4ml (2021–2022).
- LPC Distinguished Researcher, Fermilab (2015).
- European Physical Society Young Physicist Prize (2009).
