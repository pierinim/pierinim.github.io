With PhD students K. A. Woźniak and E. Puljak, and colleagues at ETH Zurich, IBM and the CERN
Quantum Technology Initiative (QTI), I worked on quantum machine learning: quantum kernel machines
and quantum clustering for anomaly detection, and quantum-inspired tensor networks, down to
low-latency implementations on FPGAs. I contributed to the CERN QTI strategy and roadmap, and in
2024–2026 I coordinated the QTI activity on quantum sensing and metrology.

Selected papers:

- [CERN Quantum Technology Initiative strategy and roadmap](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5571809) (2021)
- [Quantum anomaly detection in the latent space of proton collision events at the LHC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.10780), Commun. Phys. 7 (2024) 334
- [Unravelling physics beyond the Standard Model with classical and quantum anomaly detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.10787), Mach. Learn.: Sci. Technol. 4 (2023) 045031
- [Tensor network for anomaly detection in the latent space of proton collision events at the LHC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.00102), Mach. Learn.: Sci. Technol. 6 (2025) 045001
- [Towards tensor network models for low-latency jet tagging on FPGAs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10801), Mach. Learn.: Sci. Technol. 7 (2026) 045065
- [Quantum enhanced identification of boosted jets with quantum graph neural networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18416) (2026)
