With the first LHC data I worked on searches for supersymmetry in CMS:

- **Razor.** Together with Caltech colleagues (C. Rogan, M. Spiropulu and others) I worked on the
  razor kinematic variables (M<sub>R</sub>, R) and the CMS razor searches at 7, 8 and 13 TeV.
- **R-parity-violating SUSY with enhanced third-generation couplings**, e.g. top squarks
  decaying to final states with leptons and b jets.
- **LHC phenomenology**: how to characterize early new-physics signals ("look-alikes"),
  interpretation of SUSY searches in the pMSSM, and natural SUSY with light stops.
