I worked on CMS searches for new heavy resonances decaying to jets:

- **Dijet resonances**, with the dijet invariant-mass spectrum.
- **Diboson (VV, V = W/Z) resonances**, including boosted W/Z tagging with jet substructure.
- **Anomaly detection in dijet events**: model-agnostic searches for dijet resonances with
  anomalous jet substructure.

Selected papers (CMS Collaboration):

- [Search for exotic resonances decaying into WZ/ZZ at √s = 7 TeV](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.5779), JHEP 02 (2013) 036
- [Search for narrow resonances using the dijet mass spectrum at √s = 8 TeV](https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.4794), PRD 87 (2013) 114015
- [Search for massive resonances decaying into WW, WZ or ZZ bosons at 13 TeV](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.09159), JHEP 03 (2017) 162
- [Model-agnostic search for dijet resonances with anomalous jet substructure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.03747), Rep. Prog. Phys. 88 (2025) 067802
