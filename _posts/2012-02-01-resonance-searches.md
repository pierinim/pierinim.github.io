I worked on CMS searches for new heavy resonances decaying to jets:

- **Dijet resonances**, with the dijet invariant-mass spectrum.
- **Diboson (VV, V = W/Z) resonances**, including boosted W/Z tagging with jet substructure,
  and the combination of ATLAS and CMS Run-1 diboson searches.
- **Anomaly detection in dijet events**: model-agnostic searches for dijet resonances with
  anomalous jet substructure, building on community efforts such as the LHC Olympics 2020.

Selected papers:

- [Search for exotic resonances decaying into WZ/ZZ at √s = 7 TeV](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.5779) (CMS), JHEP 02 (2013) 036
- [Search for narrow resonances using the dijet mass spectrum at √s = 8 TeV](https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.4794) (CMS), PRD 87 (2013) 114015
- [Combination of Run-1 exotic searches in diboson final states at the LHC](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03371), JHEP 04 (2016) 155
- [Search for massive resonances decaying into WW, WZ or ZZ bosons at 13 TeV](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.09159) (CMS), JHEP 03 (2017) 162
- [The LHC Olympics 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.08320), Rep. Prog. Phys. 84 (2021) 124201
- [Model-agnostic search for dijet resonances with anomalous jet substructure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.03747) (CMS), Rep. Prog. Phys. 88 (2025) 067802
