From 2000 to 2007 I worked on the BaBar experiment at SLAC (PhD in Rome, then postdoc at LAL Orsay and University of Wisconsin–Madison). In parallel I was a member of the **UTfit** collaboration, which performs global fits of the CKM Unitarity Triangle in and beyond the Standard Model. I also worked on the phenomenology of charmless B decays ("charming penguins"). In 2009 I received the European Physical Society **Young Physicist Prize**.
