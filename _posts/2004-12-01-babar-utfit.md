Before the LHC, I was an experimentalist on the BaBar experiment at SLAC (University of Rome and University of Wisconsin–Madison) and a member of the **UTfit** collaboration, which performs global fits of the CKM Unitarity Triangle in and beyond the Standard Model. For this work I received the 2009 European Physical Society **Young Physicist Prize**.

- [UTfit](http://www.utfit.org)
- [The UTfit collaboration report on new physics in ΔF=2 (hep-ph/0509219)](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0509219)
