[Edge SpAIce](https://ep-news.web.cern.ch/edge-spaice-leveraging-cerns-ai-real-time-ocean-plastic-tracking-space/)
is a Horizon Europe project (2023–2026), coordinated by Agenium Space with CERN, the National
Technical University of Athens and EnduroSat. The goal is to run AI directly on board
Earth-observation satellites to detect marine plastic litter, so that only useful information
has to be sent to the ground.

CERN's contribution, in which I participate in one of the work packages, is to extend hls4ml to
convert neural networks for image segmentation into firmware for the FPGA systems-on-chip used in
space, balancing high frame rates with low power consumption.
