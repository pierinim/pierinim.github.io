**mPP** was my ERC Consolidator Grant (April 2018 – March 2023, ~1.7 M€, hosted by CERN), devoted to deep-learning applications in high-energy physics: from event selection in the trigger and anomaly detection, to graph neural networks for reconstruction and fast simulation.
