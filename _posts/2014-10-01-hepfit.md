[HEPfit](https://github.com/silvest/HEPfit) is an open-source code to combine direct and indirect
constraints on high-energy physics models, using Bayesian statistics. With the Rome group
(M. Ciuchini, L. Silvestrini, J. de Blas, C. Reina and others) I worked on global fits of
electroweak precision observables and Higgs-boson couplings, their interplay with new physics,
and projections for future colliders.

Selected papers:

- [Global Bayesian analysis of the Higgs-boson couplings](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.4204), Nucl. Part. Phys. Proc. 273–275 (2016) 834
- [Electroweak precision observables and Higgs-boson signal strengths in the SM and beyond: present and future](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.01509), JHEP 12 (2016) 135
- [HEPfit: a code for the combination of indirect and direct constraints on high energy physics models](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.14012), EPJC 80 (2020) 456
- [Global analysis of electroweak data in the Standard Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.07274), PRD 106 (2022) 033003
- [Impact of the recent measurements of the top-quark and W-boson masses on electroweak precision fits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.04204), PRL 129 (2022) 271801
