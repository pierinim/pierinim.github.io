I joined CMS when I started working at CERN in 2007. Over the years I worked on searches for new physics, both supersymmetry and exotica — including the development of the *razor* kinematic variables for SUSY searches, and resonance searches in dijet and diboson (VV) final states. In CMS I served as coordinator of the Physics Performance and Datasets (PPD) area and later in CMS Physics Coordination.
