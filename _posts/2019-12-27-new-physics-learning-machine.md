Together with theory colleagues, we develop methods that turn a neural network into a goodness-of-fit test, comparing data to a reference Standard Model expectation without assuming a specific signal hypothesis (the *New Physics Learning Machine*).

- [Learning multivariate new physics (arXiv:1912.12155)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.12155)
