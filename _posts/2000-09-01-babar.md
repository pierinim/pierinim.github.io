From 2000 to 2007 I worked on the BaBar experiment at the SLAC PEP-II B factory, during my
PhD in Rome and as a postdoc at LAL Orsay and at the University of Wisconsin–Madison.
My analyses focused on:

- **Charmless two-body B decays** (B → Kπ, ππ), branching fractions and direct CP violation,
  including the observation of direct CP violation in B<sup>0</sup> → K<sup>+</sup>π<sup>−</sup>.
- **Searches for B<sup>0</sup> → ℓ<sup>+</sup>ℓ<sup>−</sup>** (ee, μμ, eμ), sensitive to physics beyond the Standard Model.
- **Time-dependent CP asymmetries in b → s penguin modes with a K<sub>S</sub>**, such as
  B<sup>0</sup> → K<sub>S</sub>π<sup>0</sup>, probing new physics through deviations from sin2β.

Selected papers:

- [Observation of direct CP violation in B<sup>0</sup> → K<sup>+</sup>π<sup>−</sup> decays](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0407057), PRL 93 (2004) 131801
- [Measurements of CP-violating asymmetries in B<sup>0</sup> → K<sub>S</sub>π<sup>0</sup> decays](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0403001), PRL 93 (2004) 131805
- [Search for decays of B<sup>0</sup> mesons into pairs of charged leptons](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0408096), PRL 94 (2005) 221803
