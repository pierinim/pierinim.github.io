From 2000 to 2007 I worked on the BaBar experiment at the SLAC PEP-II B factory: as an
undergraduate and then PhD student in Rome (with F. Ferroni and G. Martinelli), and as a postdoc at
LAL Orsay and at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. My first BaBar paper, on the branching
fractions of charmless two-body B decays, came out of my undergraduate work. I was operation
manager of the BaBar muon detector in 2003 and convener of the charmless two-body B decays group
in 2006–2007.
My analyses focused on:

- **Charmless two-body B decays** (B → Kπ, ππ), branching fractions and direct CP violation,
  including the observation of direct CP violation in B<sup>0</sup> → K<sup>+</sup>π<sup>−</sup>.
- **Searches for B<sup>0</sup> → ℓ<sup>+</sup>ℓ<sup>−</sup>** (ee, μμ, eμ), sensitive to physics beyond the Standard Model.
- **Time-dependent CP asymmetries in b → s penguin modes with a K<sub>S</sub>**, such as
  B<sup>0</sup> → K<sub>S</sub>π<sup>0</sup>, probing new physics through deviations from sin2β.
